 A LITTLE INTELLIGENCE, YOUR CHOICE

Bring your photos
into focus.

Use the same Gemini or Qwen connection as image search. Saved image embeddings are reused. A key is kept only in this server’s memory and clears when the server restarts.

Loading a key does not validate it or make a paid request. Preparing and searching photos send data to your chosen provider; their billing applies. Switching providers needs that provider’s own image embeddings.

Your real photos have their own space

“My trips” saves collections from this workspace. Image search in the original workspace already uses real Gemini or Qwen embeddings. New trip statistics and rankings only use recorded annotations, with their source shown.

Start with a selection, keep control

The iOS app supports selecting photos through Apple’s Photos access. Automatic iCloud or shared-album syncing is not connected. An export here downloads a selection manifest; it never messages friends or deletes photos.
