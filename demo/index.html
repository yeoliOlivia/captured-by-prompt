Captured by Prompt

What do you want to find?

This demo searches a set of public sample photos (COCO128 and scikit-image). In the real app, your own photos stay on your computer.

Stricter

Search ranks every photo by how close it is to your words. The strictness slider decides how many of the closest ones to show.

Sample collection

Captured by Prompt is an early prototype. The full app also understands the people, places and trips in your life ("Mark and me on the Japan trip"), and runs on your own computer with your own photos. Code and feedback on GitHub.

Photo credits: COCO128 (Ultralytics, from the COCO dataset) and the scikit-image sample collection. Search uses Google's gemini-embedding-2; example searches were computed ahead of time, so this page sends nothing to any model.
